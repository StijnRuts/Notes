# ThinkPad X201

## About

The ThinkPad X201 is a 12.1-inch laptop released by Lenovo in 2010, with an Intel Core i5 or i7 processor and BIOS-only firmware.
Because the firmware is BIOS-only, GRUB is installed on `/dev/sda` and boots from the `/boot` partition.
