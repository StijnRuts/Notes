# Zettelkasten

## About

Zettelkasten is a personal knowledge management method built around many small, atomic notes linked to one another.
Each note captures one idea in its own words, gets a unique identifier, and points to related notes;
structure emerges from the links rather than from folders.
Popularized by sociologist Niklas Luhmann, whose slip-box grew to tens of thousands of notes, the method is intended to help ideas accumulate and combine over time.
This [[digital-garden]] uses [[zk]] to maintain a Zettelkasten-style notebook of linked notes.
